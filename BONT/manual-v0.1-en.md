## 🌟 THE GOLDEN RULE (Your daily mantra)

> Something wrong? Don't panic.

1. **SET ASIDE** the problematic product (to the side, without blocking the flow).
2. **KEEP** working with the next one (flow is sacred).
3. **CHECK** what you set aside when the pace slows down (during low-load moments).

> *"Quality before speed (but with champion pace)"*
>
> ⚠️ *[PENDING OFFICIAL VALIDATION: The loading criteria described here are based on daily operational experience. They do not replace DHL's official quality and safety standards nor the instructions of the teamleaders.]*

Loading an RCG or pallet is like playing Tetris in real life. Each piece must go in its place so the customer receives their product perfectly.

### 🏆 What IS done (Hall of Fame)

- **The Tetris Master:** RCG with boxes snugly fitted, no dangerous gaps.
- **Elite Pallet of Krats:** Krats aligned, labels facing outward.
- **Smart mixed assembly:** Heavy base at the bottom, pipes protected on the sides, light boxes on top.

### ❌ What is NOT done (Avoid disaster)

| Error | Description | Solution | Status |
|-------|-------------|----------|--------|
| 🚫 Product protrudes from RCG | Outside the metal perimeter. | Relocate inside. If it doesn't fit, use another RCG. | Pending official validation |
| 📏 Height > 2.3 m | Risk of tipping or damage. | Measure visually. Redistribute. | Pending official validation |
| ⬆️ Arrows ignored | Box with arrow pointing down or sideways. | Rotate until arrow points up. No arrow = fragile. | Pending official validation |
| 🎩 Hat effect | Large box on top of small one. | Base ≥ top box; heavy at bottom. | Pending official validation |
| 🔧 Pipes on top of boxes | Pipes resting on cardboard. | Always at the back or side of the RCG. | Pending official validation |
| 📋 Krats with labels facing inward | Codes hidden. | Stickers visible facing outward. | Pending official validation |

### 🔍 INVENTORY DSP Protocol (for resolving set-aside products)

> ⚠️ *[PENDING OFFICIAL VALIDATION: The INVENTORY DSP procedure, menu names, keyboard shortcuts (such as "down arrow 8 times"), and the interpretation of codes described here are empirical findings from daily operations. They have not been confirmed as the official methodology by DHL or Blue Yonder.]*

1. In Blue Yonder → three dots (...) → **TOOLS** → **INVENTORY DSP**.
2. Scan the product.
3. **PRO TIP:** Press down arrow 8 times to see all the info.
4. Interpret the codes:

| INVENTORY DSP Code | Meaning | Action | Status |
|--------------------|---------|--------|--------|
| **rolpalbc** | OPEN container. Look for the number and deposit WITHOUT SCANNING. | Find the container and place the product without using the scanner. | **[PENDING OFFICIAL VALIDATION]** |
| **PND-A** | Product READY for today. Open a new RCG/pallet or look for its own. | Open a new container or find the one already assigned. | **[PENDING OFFICIAL VALIDATION]** |
| **PND-P** | CLOSED RCG or pallet. Look for the number and deposit WITHOUT SCANNING. | Find the closed container and place. | **[PENDING OFFICIAL VALIDATION]** |
| **T3-STGE** | Already in truck line. Take it to the loading area (GC). | Take the product to the loading area (GC). | **[PENDING OFFICIAL VALIDATION]** |

### ⚡ Scanner not responding – step-by-step solution

> ⚠️ *[PENDING OFFICIAL VALIDATION: Procedure based on personal experience. DHL may have a different technical support protocol.]*

1. Adjust distance and clean the label.
2. If it doesn't work, **SET ASIDE** the product.
3. When load decreases, enter the **SSCC manually** (it is printed).
4. If it still doesn't work or the code is damaged, inform **Teamleaders**.

## 🟢 GREEN CATEGORY – Smart Scanner, labels and printer

> ⚠️ *[PENDING OFFICIAL VALIDATION: The Smart Scanner procedures, INBOUND/SURPLUS methods, and labeling protocols described here are based on daily operational practice. They have not been validated as official procedures by DHL or the Blue Yonder system administrators.]*

### 📦 PART 1: Product entry (INBOUND / SURPLUS)

Use **Smart Scanner** when the normal scanner says "NOT FOUND" or "SSCC number is wrong or missing".

#### INBOUND Method

> ⚠️ *[PENDING OFFICIAL VALIDATION: Codes such as KRT, COL and their use in INBOUND are tentative.]*

1. Scan the lower code (SSCC).
2. Choose the type (KRT, COL…).
3. If it says **"NOT FOUND"**, switch to SURPLUS.

#### SURPLUS Method

> ⚠️ *[PENDING OFFICIAL VALIDATION: The INBOUND → SURPLUS → INBOUND flow described here is an empirical finding.]*

1. Switch to **SURPLUS** mode.
2. Scan the lower code + upper code (client).
3. If it says **"ANOTHER POSITION"**, go back to INBOUND.

### 🖨️ PART 2: LPN label management, printer and closing

> ⚠️ *[PENDING OFFICIAL VALIDATION: Labeling protocols A, B and C, including the use of CHANGE POSITION and SSCC INTERGAMMA, are empirical solutions developed in daily operations. It is not confirmed that they are the official methods or that they have been approved by the system administrators.]*

#### PROTOCOL A – Retrieve LPN to continue loading

⚠️ **Only works if you have a scanned product** (screen asks for destination LPN).

1. Blue Yonder → three dots → **CHANGE POSITION**.
2. Down arrow → LPN appears.
3. **Write it down on paper** (cannot be copied to Smart Scanner).
4. Smart Scanner → **PRINTING** → select your printer → **LPN PRINTING** → enter code manually.

### 🎨 Paint or liquid spill (Critical mission)

> ⚠️ *[PENDING OFFICIAL VALIDATION: The emergency kit, its location, the type of sand, and the cleaning procedure must be validated against DHL's official spill handling protocols. Do not assume this procedure is the official one.]*

2. Go to the **emergency kit (sand)** and spread it over the spill.
3. Clean **from the outside inward**.
4. Dispose of waste in **special containers**.

### ❌ Container mix-up (frequent human error)

> ⚠️ *[PENDING OFFICIAL VALIDATION: This error and its solution are based on experience. DHL may have a different formal procedure for correcting assignment errors.]*

- **Symptom:** You scan the LPN of an RCG/pallet that does not match the destination.
- **Action:** Verify destination on screen, compare numbers, relocate product.
- If it's a repeated pattern, inform Teamleaders.

### ❌ Scanning a product without placing it (bad habit)

> ⚠️ *[PENDING OFFICIAL VALIDATION: The consequence described ("the system registers the product as placed") and the proposed solution are theories based on experience. The exact system behavior must be validated against Blue Yonder's official documentation.]*

- **Consequence:** The system registers the product as "placed" (but it isn't). Later another operator scans the same product and gets an error.
- **Prevention:** Never scan without placing. If the RCG is full, close it, replace with an empty one, then scan.
- **If it happened:** Try to place it; if there's no space, inform Teamleaders.

### 👀 You find a product in the wrong place

1. Scan to see the destination.
2. If possible, place it where indicated.
3. If not, use **INVENTORY DSP** (it may be in a neighboring container).
4. If it's on the floor, consult inventory dsp.
5. If you see a repeated pattern, inform Teamleaders.
